 1. GETTING STARTED
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ① Updating? Quit the old one first:
      right-click the tray icon (bottom-right, maybe under the ^ arrow)
      → Quit.

   ② Double-click  AkraPlayer.exe
      Your keys, profiles and settings carry over.

   ③ "Windows protected your PC"?  →  More info  →  Run anyway.
      (The app isn't signed, so Windows asks the first time.)

   ④ Want YouTube play/pause too? See section 9 or the guide inside the
      "AkraPlayer YouTube Extension" folder.

 2. THE PLAYER AT A GLANCE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   +--------------------------------------------------------------+
   | (A) Akra Player  (moon 23m)    [pin][mini][-][+][x]          |
   |                                                              |
   |   +--------+        Song title                     (heart)   |
   |   | cover  |        Artist                                   |
   |   +--------+        1:24 =======O---------------- 3:20       |
   |                        |<<     ||     >>|          (speaker) |
   |                                                              |
   | [gear] [cover] [color]   Ctrl + Shift + 7   [keys] [mixer]   |
   |                            Declutter                         |
   +--------------------------------------------------------------+

   Drag the glass ............ move the player anywhere
   Scroll down on it ......... opens Settings
   Click the song / cover .... jump to Spotify
   |<<   ||   >>| ............ previous / play-pause / next
   Heart ..................... like the song in Spotify
   Speaker ................... mute Spotify (hover it for its volume bar)
   3:20 (the length) ......... click to show time left (-1:56)
   Moon 23m .................. the sleep timer is running (click it)

   Bottom left ............... Settings, cover on/off, color
   Bottom right .............. your keystrokes, the mixer
   Declutter ................. hides everything but the essentials
                               ("Show" brings it all back)

   Top right ................. pin (always on top) · mini player ·
                               shrink · enlarge · close

 3. KEYS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   GLOBAL HOTKEYS (work anywhere, even in games)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────
   Set them in  Settings › Keybinds.  Some favorites:

     Play / pause Spotify     Play / pause YouTube    Like song
     Play / pause window      Mute current window     Open Akra Player
     Volume Toggle            Hold to lower music     Declutter

   Setting a key:
     • Click "Change" on a keybind, then press the new key.
     • F-keys, media keys, letters, mouse buttons 3–5 all work.
     • Ctrl / Alt / Shift are recommended, not required. If a key would
       take over something common, a yellow note tells you what.
     • Esc cancels · Backspace removes the key.

   WHILE THE PLAYER IS IN FRONT
   ────────────────────────────
     Space ........ play / pause Spotify
     Left / Right . previous / next song
     Up / Down .... Spotify volume (5% a press)
     M ............ mute / unmute Spotify
     Esc .......... close what's open, or hide the player

 4. SIZE & MINI PLAYER
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   Drag a corner ......... resize; everything rearranges to fit
   Enlarge / shrink ...... one step bigger or smaller per click
   Hold enlarge 1.5 s .... fill the screen (above the taskbar)
   Hold shrink 1.5 s ..... back to the normal size
   Mini player ........... a small pill in the nearest corner;
                           its square button brings the player back

 5. VISUALIZER                          Settings › Appearance › Background
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   Turn on "Visualizer", then pick a style:

     Bars ......... along the bottom of the player
     Halo ......... a ring of light in the middle
     Screen Edge .. along the bottom of a screen (bars or a waveform),
                    even with the player hidden; clicks go through it
     Threads ...... hundreds of thin strands moving together
     Glow Line .... one smooth glowing line
     Mesh ......... a dotted 3D blob that pulses
     Ripples ...... rings spreading out on every beat
     Waveform ..... a mirrored waveform, newest sound in the middle
     Sparks ....... embers rising faster when it gets loud

   Fine-tune it underneath:
     • Sensitivity ... how strongly it reacts
     • Smoothness .... calm and flowing, or snappy
     • Each style's own sliders (size, amount, glow… the middle = default)
     • Waveform colors: Theme · Custom · RGB · Gradient · Liquid

 6. MIXER, KEYSTROKES & SLEEP TIMER
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   MIXER  (sliders button, bottom right)
     Every app playing sound, each with its own volume slider.
     Click an app's icon to mute it.

   KEYSTROKES  (keyboard button, bottom right)
     All your hotkeys, ready to change.

   Both open in their own glass window. Drag it anywhere.
     • Click somewhere else and it closes.
     • Pin it and it stays open, even when you close the player.

   SLEEP TIMER  (Settings › General, or the tray menu)
     Off · 15 min · 30 min · 1 hour · End of song.
     Spotify pauses when it's up, even with the player hidden.

 7. SETTINGS TOUR
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   Tip: the pop-out button (top right of Settings) opens Settings in its
   own window, so you can watch your changes on the player.
   Hover anything for a short explanation.

   GENERAL
     • Open on startup · Always on top · Album art · Tooltips
     • Key-press popups ..... a note when a hotkey does something
                              (choose its corner)
     • Now playing popups ... the next song, while the player is hidden
                              (pencil: choose the screen and corner)
     • Fade when idle ....... the player fades while you're not using it
                              (pencil: how long, and how faint)
     • Sleep timer · scroll resistance · Export & import
     • Reset to default ..... click twice (keeps keybinds and profiles)

   APPEARANCE
     • Profiles ............. Default, Glass, or save your own with +
     • Accent color ......... Blue · Red · Green · Yellow · Orange ·
                              Black/White (click again to switch) ·
                              RGB shift · Album cover · Custom
     • Glass clarity
     • Background ▾ ......... blur · outline · fluid background ·
                              visualizer
     • Cover glow · Resize around the button · Colored tray icon
     • Switched-on look ..... Edge glow · Frosted fill · Classic
     • Font

   KEYBINDS
     • Profiles ............. e.g. "Gaming" and "Studying"; one click to
                              switch (also from the tray menu)
     • Folders .............. Audio · Volume · Player (rename, collapse,
                              add your own)
     • Every hotkey is a card. Play/pause window and YouTube have an
       on/off switch in their bottom-right corner.

 8. EXPORT & IMPORT                               Settings › General
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   EXPORT…
     ① Pick what to include: General · Appearance · Keybinds
     ② Click Export.
        It's saved in the "Exported Profiles" folder (next to
        AkraPlayer.exe) as  "Akra Player Export - <profile name>".
        "Change…" saves it somewhere else.

   IMPORT…
     ① Pick an export from the list (or Browse… for one elsewhere).
     ② Tick the parts you want.
     ③ Click Import. Those parts replace your current ones.

 10. GOOD TO KNOW
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ▸ Like song
     Spotify doesn't let apps like songs, so Akra Player presses
     Spotify's own shortcut (Alt+Shift+B) inside Spotify for you. From a
     hotkey it skips this while a fullscreen game is in front (it would
     minimize the game). Use the heart instead.

   ▸ Popups over games
     They show in borderless / windowed mode. Exclusive fullscreen games
     don't let anything draw over them.

   ▸ Background blur & screenshots
     To blur what's behind it, the glass hides from a quick screen grab
     for a split second. A screenshot taken in exactly that moment may
     miss the player; just take it again.

   ▸ Hold to lower music
     Hold a key (like your Discord push-to-talk key) and your music drops
     until you let go. The key still reaches Discord.

   ▸ Old icon still showing?
     That's Windows' icon cache. It updates on its own (or after a
     restart).
